# Privacy Policy for Know Yourself
Last updated: November 2026

Know Yourself ("the App") is designed with a strict privacy-first, on-device architecture.

1. Information We Process:
- Voice Recordings & Audio: Recorded solely when you tap the microphone button. Audio is transcribed and analyzed locally on your device.
- Journal Entries & Reflections: Your thoughts, transcripts, tags, and emotional metrics are saved exclusively to local device storage.
- Biometrics: If you enable biometric app lock, verification is performed securely by your device operating system (Android BiometricPrompt). We never receive or store biometric credentials.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties:
- We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your voice recordings, transcripts, or personal reflections to any third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Retention and Deletion:
- All data remains on your physical device. You can permanently delete any entry or purge all app data at any time from within the application or via Android system settings.
